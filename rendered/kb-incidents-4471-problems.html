parser · #1

The shared parser reads every date month first. All our tenants write the day first, so any date with a day of 12 or less comes out with day and month swapped: 7 April goes out as 4 July. This is the bug #4471 was opened for, and the queued fix. It reaches 11 tenants through the app, email and the booking-v2 form.

hint · #2

The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what the hint tells them, month first, and the form reads the field the tenant’s way, day first. The symptom is the same swap, so the reports look like the parser bug, but the fix is in the form. A parser fix does not touch it. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants (3 tenants) land here, whatever intake tagged them.

one-day-move · #3

These dates are not swapped. They move by one day: Tuesday 6 October shows as Monday, a client in Lisbon gets Tuesday while Toronto gets Monday, an invite arrives before the booking was made. The desk already read them this way (desk-notes.json), and CauseTable footnotes them under parser, where they are filed and still counted. That points at time zone handling, not the parser, so they need their own ticket. They count there too.
